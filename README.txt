SBOM FIXER DASHBOARD
====================

Double-click SbomDashboard.exe (keep the black window open). Your browser opens at
http://127.0.0.1:8765 . Nothing needs to be installed; it works without internet.

Options:  SbomDashboard.exe --port 9000 | --no-browser | --host 0.0.0.0

Keep the whole folder together. Copy the whole folder to move it.
